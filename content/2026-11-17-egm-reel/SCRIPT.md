## 1. Package
- **Idea in one sentence:** the AI that helped you write a plan is the worst one to check it, so Google's new open-source plugin hands your plan to reviewers that remember nothing.
- **Common lenses (avoided):** "Google releases a new AI coding plugin" news recap · "spec-driven development" tutorial · "best Claude Code plugins" listicle.
- **Our lens:** *forgetting is the feature.* A reviewer that knows too much fills your gaps from memory; a goldfish can't.
- **Format steal:** paradox hook ("better because it forgets") → "here's why" → one concrete example the viewer can check themselves → payoff.
- **Titles:** "Google built an AI whose superpower is forgetting" · "Why your AI can't review its own plan" · "The elephant and the goldfish: Google's new AI review trick"
- **First frame:** an elephant and a goldfish face to face, the line "Its superpower: forgetting." already on screen.
