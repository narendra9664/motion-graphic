[excited] Google just open-sourced an AI reviewer whose superpower is... forgetting. Because the AI that helped you write a plan is the worst one to check it. It already knows what you meant... so it never notices what you didn't write. That's the idea behind the Elephant-Goldfish Model. Over twenty thousand stars in under a month. The elephant remembers everything: your project, your goals, every decision. It drafts the plan with you. Then the goldfish read it. Fresh reviewers with zero memory of your conversation. All they get is the page. Picture a plan that says: "Volunteers staff the desk. We keep the Friday rule." You'd nod. A goldfish asks: which volunteers? What hours? What Friday rule? Every gap the elephant was quietly filling in from memory. And you stay in charge: you pick which findings matter, and you approve the design before your agent writes a line of code. It runs as a plugin in Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT and Antigravity. Free and open source. Follow for one repo like this every week. Because sometimes the smartest thing an AI can do... is forget.
