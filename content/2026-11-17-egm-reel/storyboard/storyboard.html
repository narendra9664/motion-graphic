Elephant-Goldfish reel · ~60 s · 9:16 · storyboard · Liam · new look

Look: EGM's own colours. Deep navy ground with a soft blue glow (elephant side) and orange glow (goldfish side), white paper cards, rounded geometric sans (Figtree). Two recurring characters: the elephant (remembers everything, writes the plan) and the goldfish (remembers nothing, reads only the page); both react on every beat (blink, tilt, bounce). Official EGM marks only on the brand card. Same house rules: one forward camera, each shot's key object becomes the next shot, action dead centre (red crosshair), soft background, words slide in. Dashed lines = Reels text-safe band. Blue = voiceover, yellow = what moves, green = how it becomes the next shot.
