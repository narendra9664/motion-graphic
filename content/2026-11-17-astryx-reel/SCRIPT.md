- **Idea in one sentence:** Meta kept the design system behind 13,000 of its apps inside for eight years, and the version it just open-sourced is built for your AI agent, not just you.

- **Format steal:** "X just gave away the thing behind Y" → "but that's not the interesting part" → the real twist.
